You'll open a message sent on Vara mainnet and find out exactly what it asked a program to do, and what happened.

<ReadOnlyNote />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Message Inspector">
    Go to [hexkit.app/vara/transactions?tool=message](https://hexkit.app/vara/transactions?tool=message).

    From anywhere else in HexKit, select **Vara** in the chain switcher at the top left, then **Programs & messages → Message Inspector**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect a message">
    Enter `36589852-1` and select **Inspect**.

    `36589852-1` means extrinsic 1 in block 36,589,852. The field also accepts a Subscan or Gear IDEA link, or a message ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary">
    After a few seconds the summary shows the call `gear.sendMessage` marked **Success**, sent to the **Polybaskets** program (`0xa749cc…2cba`).

    The **IDL from Gear IDEA** badge means HexKit found the program's Sails interface and used it to decode the message.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/vara-message-inspector.png" alt="Message Inspector showing message 36589852-1 decoded as BasketMarket.FinalizeSettlement with basket_id 1633" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read what the message did">
    - **Payload** shows the call: `BasketMarket.FinalizeSettlement` with `basket_id: 1633`.
    - **Events** shows what the program emitted in the same block: `BasketMarket.SettlementFinalized` for basket `1633`, with a `payout_per_share` of `10000`.
    - The **Diagnosis** tab confirms the message was included, processed, and answered with a successful reply code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can now say what this message did without reading any hex: it asked Polybaskets to finalize the settlement for basket 1633, and the program did.

To keep a copy, open **Investigation** and choose **Save in this browser** or **Export as a file**. An exported file opens again later with **Open an investigation file…**, even offline.
